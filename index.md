# Privacy Policy for Magnifier

**Last updated:** 13 October 2026

This policy explains what Magnifier ("the app") does with your information.
Magnifier is developed by APP2GO ("we", "us").

The short version: **the app has no accounts and no sign-in, and we never see
your photos or anything the camera reads.** Those stay on your device. Google
collects limited technical data to show ads, process purchases and report
crashes, and — only where you have agreed to it — to tell us which parts of
the app are being used. Some ads are supplied by another advertising network
through Google, which receives the same technical data. All of that is
described in full below.

---

## 1. What stays on your device

The following never leaves your phone and is never sent to us or anyone else:

- **Camera images.** The live camera view is processed entirely on your
  device. Frames are not uploaded, stored, or transmitted.
- **Recognized text.** When you use the text recognition feature, the
  reading is performed by an on-device model that is included in the app
  itself. It works with no internet connection, and the text is never sent
  anywhere. It is shown to you on screen, and copied to your clipboard only
  when you press the copy button.
- **Photos you take.** Photos are saved to your device's storage and to your
  system photo gallery. We have no access to them.
- **Your free-tier usage count.** The app stores a small local record of how
  many text scans you have used in the past 24 hours, so it can apply the
  free-tier limit. It contains no identifying information and is deleted
  when you uninstall the app.
- **A 3-day Pro pass.** If you choose to watch an ad for 3 days of Pro, the
  app records on your device when the pass started and when it ends. Nothing
  else is stored, and it is deleted when the pass ends or you uninstall the
  app.

## 2. Data collected by third parties

Magnifier uses five Google services, and one advertising network that Google
passes ad requests on to. Each collects data directly, under its own privacy
policy.

### Your consent comes first

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and other regions where
the law requires it, the app shows you a Google consent form the first time
you open it. **Advertising and usage analytics both wait for your answer.**
If you do not agree, usage analytics are not collected at all, and ads are
limited accordingly.

You can change your mind at any time: open **Settings** in the app and choose
**Privacy options**. Changes take effect the next time you open the app.

Where the law does not require a consent form, none is shown and the
collection described below applies from first use.

### Advertising — Google AdMob

Free users are shown ads. To serve and measure them, Google may collect:

- your device's advertising identifier
- device information (model, operating system version, language, screen size)
- approximate location derived from your IP address
- interactions with ads shown in the app

Google may use this to personalize ads. You can limit this in your device
settings under **Settings → Privacy → Ads**, where you can reset or delete
your advertising ID and opt out of ad personalization.

Google's advertising privacy information:
https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

**People with Pro do not see ads** — whether they bought it or are using a
3-day pass — and no ad requests are made while Pro is active. If you choose
to watch an ad for a 3-day pass, that ad is served by AdMob like any other,
and only after you have confirmed you want to watch it.

### Ads from other networks — Liftoff Monetize

AdMob does not always have an advertiser for a given ad request. When it does
not, it offers that request to other advertising networks, so the space can
still be filled. The only network enabled is **Liftoff Monetize** (also known
as Vungle).

Where Liftoff supplies an ad, it receives the same categories of data listed
above — your advertising identifier, device information, approximate location
derived from your IP address, and how you interacted with the ad — and
handles that data under its own privacy policy:

https://liftoff.io/privacy-policy/

**Your consent choice covers this network too.** If you decline in the form
described above, or withdraw later through **Settings → Privacy options**,
Liftoff is limited in exactly the same way AdMob is. People with Pro, who see
no ads at all, are never subject to any of it.

Nothing additional is collected for this: no photos, no recognized text, and
nothing the camera has read ever reaches an advertising network, any more
than it reaches us.

### Usage analytics — Google Analytics for Firebase

So that we can see which parts of the app people actually use, and where they
get stuck, the app reports a small number of events to Google. **Nothing is
reported unless you have agreed to it**, as described above.

The events are deliberately few, and this is all of them:

- finishing the short introduction the first time you open the app
- opening the magnifier
- opening the app again on the day after you first opened it
- the first button you press after the magnifier opens
- which feature you used — brightness, filters, taking a photo, text
  scanning, or the gallery
- seeing the Pro offer
- pressing the button to buy Pro, and what happened next — whether it went
  through, was cancelled, failed, or is still pending
- buying Pro, with the price paid and its currency
- closing the Pro offer without buying, and whether you had got as far as
  Google's payment screen
- choosing to watch an ad for 3 days of Pro, and how that ended — whether
  the pass was granted, the ad was closed early, you changed your mind, or
  no ad was available

Alongside those, Google collects:

- a randomly generated app-installation identifier
- your device's advertising identifier
- device model, operating system version, app version, and language
- the country your IP address resolves to

We see the result only as counts and charts — how many people did a thing,
not who. **It contains no photos, no recognized text, and nothing the camera
has read.** We cannot identify you from it, and we do not try to.

Firebase privacy information: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy

### Crash reporting — Firebase Crashlytics

When the app crashes or hits an unexpected error, a report is sent so the
problem can be fixed. A report contains:

- the technical stack trace of the error
- device model, operating system version and app version
- a randomly generated installation identifier

Reports contain no photos, no recognized text, and nothing you have typed or
read through the app.

Firebase privacy information: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy

### App settings — Firebase Remote Config

When the app starts, it asks Firebase Remote Config for settings we can
change without releasing an update. At present there is one: whether ads are
shown at all. The request carries a randomly generated installation
identifier and basic information about the app and device, such as the app
version and language — nothing you have photographed, read or typed.

This request is made whether or not you agree to analytics, because it
collects nothing about how you use the app: it only decides whether the app
shows ads. When ads are switched off, the app makes no requests to Google
AdMob or Liftoff at all.

Firebase privacy information: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy

### Purchases — Google Play Billing

Magnifier Pro is a one-time purchase. If you buy it, the purchase is handled
entirely by Google Play. We receive only confirmation that you own it, and
the order number Google gives the purchase. **We never see your name, payment
card, or billing address.**

Earlier versions of the app sold Pro as a weekly subscription. If you still
have one, you can manage or cancel it at any time through the Google Play
Store app under **Subscriptions**.

## 3. Permissions the app requests

| Permission | Why it is needed |
|---|---|
| Camera | The core function of the app — magnifying what the camera sees. Without it the app cannot work. |
| Photos / storage | To save the photos you choose to take, and to show them in the in-app gallery. |
| Flashlight | To turn on the torch when you tap the flashlight button. |

The app does not request contacts, microphone, precise location, or access to
files other than the photos it saves itself.

## 4. Children

Magnifier is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly
collect information from them.

## 5. Your rights

We hold no information that identifies you — the usage statistics described
in section 2 reach us only as aggregate counts, with no way to trace them
back to a person. There is therefore nothing for us to retrieve, correct, or
delete on your behalf.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or a
jurisdiction with comparable law (such as California), your rights of access,
correction, deletion, and objection apply to the data handled by Google and by
Liftoff Monetize as described in section 2. Those requests should be directed
to whichever of them holds the data, using the links above, or exercised
through your device's privacy settings.

To stop usage analytics being collected, withdraw your consent through
**Settings → Privacy options** in the app.

To remove everything the app has placed on your device, uninstall it. Photos
you have already saved to your gallery remain there, and can be deleted like
any other photo.
